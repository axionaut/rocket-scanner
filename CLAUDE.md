This document is the single source of truth for how Rocket Scanner works **today**: architecture, trading rules and the release workflow. Keep it current: when a release changes behaviour, edit the section it changes; do not append a changelog here.

## Mandatory Multi-Agent Alignment & Verification Protocol - 9 November 2026

- **Every implementation must be verified with multiple specialized subagents/agents.**
- Before writing, modifying, or deploying user-facing strategies or core logic, run a multi-agent critique loop:
  1. **Intent & Transcript Auditor:** Audits the conversation history from Step 0 to ensure 100% alignment with the user's explicit prompts, instructions, and constraints (no invented rules, no hallucinations).
  2. **Data & Experiment Verifier:** Audits the actual empirical math, scripts, and backtest results to ensure numbers and trade mechanics are mathematically true and grounded.
  3. **Implementation & Architecture Critic:** Reviews live code line by line to detect flaws, gate locks, whipsaws, or divergence between empirical research and production.
- All agents must cross-critique each other until a 100% unanimous consensus is achieved before finalizing code changes.
- Skipping this multi-agent alignment protocol is forbidden.

## 1. Current system

v1680 (owner 11 Nov): Unified Profit-Underwritten Opportunity Swap (PUOS) architecture. Standalone loss trims to idle cash are retired; no standalone GTT sell orders are placed to sell losers into cash. The 5-session net profit pool (configured via `Loss trim budget %` in the top bar) acts strictly as the underwriting risk buffer for Opportunity Swaps. An Opportunity Swap is only permitted if the sacrifice loss is fully covered by available booked net profit (`LOSS_BUDGET.available()`). An asymmetric expectancy hurdle is enforced: the incoming buyer's potential target gain must be >= the sacrifice's realized loss; if the sacrifice's loss would exceed the buyer's potential target gain, the sacrifice is resized to a smaller partial trim so the swap is net positive upon reaching target. When booked net profit is zero, loss-bearing swaps are completely blocked to preserve account principal. Manual Buy Basket and Kite Auto-Send buttons are retired.

Loss budget (current v1683 calculation): the saved Loss trim budget percentage underwrites Opportunity Swaps only. Its base is the previous four trading sessions' booked net plus today's booked winning net, floored at zero; apply the saved percentage, subtract today's booked losses in full, then subtract outstanding reservations and the Rs5 cushion. This is the existing calculation, not a change to the owner's setting. Missing session history or unvalued reservations hold new trimming without discarding an active trigger. Active GTT reservations are preserved through rollover, reconstructed from broker GTTs and repriced without releasing the old reservation first. The current pool uses RS operational weighted cost and estimated fees. It is not reconciled to Zerodha Console FIFO; the user's verified November P&L screenshot differs and has no day-wise/charge breakdown. Do not describe this pool as broker-confirmed P&L. The current research snapshot records the selected percentage and replay uses that recorded setting.

**StratGen replay performance (5 Nov service fix):** the research backtest spends most of its time in the shared account/exit replay, not in scoring. Two measured costs were removed. The replay's minute loader parsed each symbol's entire Historical_1min file with the generic quoted-CSV reader and `Date.parse` per row; it now reads plain rows, stops at the window start (the file is newest-first) and skips days past the end. The 10-second loss check rebuilt the whole day's book on every call; the book is now cached until a fill, add or day roll changes it, and the check returns before touching the ledger when no trim is possible. Measured on a real-candidate replay: 1 month 38.3 s → 3.5 s, 3 months 95 s → 16.9 s, identical trades (41 / 84). A completed generate cycle is now dominated by the ~3-minute symbol simulation and should return to the old multi-per-hour rate.

**StratGen live evaluator scheduling (v1635):** live evaluation loads only the recent 45 calendar days of minutes (the RVOL and normal-bar windows need ~20 sessions), cutting a symbol from ~1.7 s to ~0.4 s with identical output. Symbols are re-evaluated at their contract's next completed bar, not every tick; a signal is valid until the next bar closes plus 30 seconds, with 90 seconds as the floor for 1-minute work (`strategy.signalTtlMs`). The per-tick batch is sized from the due set (20 to 60 symbols), and `/api/stratgen/status` exposes `candidates`, `batch` and `lastTickMs` so coverage is measurable. A 1-minute contract over a very large candidate set still cannot be swept inside its signal life; that limit is visible in those counters rather than hidden.

**Dynamic MLoT trailing exit without upper ceiling (v1621, owner 4 Nov):** the exit upper target leg is removed; sell GTTs are single-leg trailing stop-loss orders (`type: 'single'`), leaving upside unconstrained. Arming occurs when price clears delivery charges plus DP (`down(ltp - minGap) >= breakeven`). The ratchet adapts dynamically to live MLoT quantiles computed across rolling sessions in real time: Stage 1 (Breakeven Lock) holds the stop firmly at breakeven while gain is below the live 25th percentile (p25), preventing microsecond tick-shakeouts within the noise floor; Stage 2 (Active Trailing) trails new highs by the empirical regime gap (p50 - p25, at least 0.26%); Stage 3 (Extension Tightening) kicks in past p75 to lock in peak profits before mean reversion. Neither leg ever drops.

**Same-day addition protection (v1620, owner 4 Nov; retained for loss-bearing Opportunity Swaps):** any filled same-day CNC buy into a carried opening holding blocks profit-funded loss trims for that stock until the next IST session, regardless of amount or signal source. Pending/unfilled buys do not qualify; new positions with no carried opening quantity retain existing exits. Entry/scoring rules and ordinary profit-protecting exits are unchanged.

The wait lasts while the score stays at/above the floor; below it, the stock is disarmed until a new crossing. Crossings before 09:15 never arm. ## 4. Critical System Invariants (DO NOT VIOLATE)

1. **No Over-Engineering**: Do not re-introduce multi-sample websocket trend checks (e.g. `diff > prior.diff` across rungs) that block the entire universe from qualifying.
2. **Re-crossings can add to held positions (owner confirmed 5 Oct 2026).** The former no-averaging-down wording is obsolete. A purchase consumes its crossing; a completed engine run must explicitly observe the score below the floor before another crossing can buy again, held or not. Waiting/fired dip orders and unresolved exit cleanup retain their existing guards. Do not reintroduce a held-stock veto. See `executeAutoBuyOrders` and `resetAutoBuyCrossings` in `dev/server.js`.
3. **Exits: profit-funded partial loss trims on any holding below cost, worst first, funded from the last 5 sessions, no time limit (owner 16 Oct v1514; v1603 30 Oct).** Keep the OCO bracket exit above. A holding becomes trim-eligible only below the current learned loss cutoff (initially 1.25%, `LOSS_TRIGGER_PCT` in dev/loss-budget.cjs; replaced only by a validated loss-cutoff learner result), and a trim is funded only from today's booked net profit as described in section 1. The former -6% deep line (v1509/v1514) is retired. Never fall back to an unconditional full loss sale. The remainder stays held; this protects the estimated booked-profit pool, not the mark-to-market value of held shares. Do not add a holding-age deadline or silently change saved settings.

4. **CLAUDE.md is a spec, not a log.** Release notes go to `docs/RELEASE_HISTORY.md` (v1386 onward) or `docs/CHANGELOG_ARCHIVE.md` (v1-v1385); both are local only because they contain trades and P&L and the repo is public.
5. **Only a live, current-session, successful ranking can produce GO, funding or a basket order.** A replay, dry-run, other-session or failed engine file never can. The qualifying event is a stock's live score crossing the floor, not the file's stage (v1418); the 17 Sep loss (0 of 5 provisional names survived to the final) is why a stage or a list is never a licence to buy wholesale.
6. **Never state an agreement/accuracy rate that has not been measured.** The 17 Sep loss came from an agent's "provisional and final usually agree closely", invented on the spot. If the number isn't in a test or a backtest, say it is unmeasured.
7. **Never serve a file with a bare `.pipe(res)`** (v1399): an aborted request leaks the descriptor, and on Windows a leaked handle blocks `os.replace` **forever** — that is how the preview ranking froze for 97 minutes on 18 Sep while completed runs stranded as `.tmp`. Destroy the stream on client `close`/`error`. Every atomic write in the engine goes through `publish()`, never a raw `os.replace`.
8. **The basket file is a snapshot, not a live mirror** (v1402; v1426: the snapshot is kept only while current capital can still pay for it - a basket that costs more than the available capital is spent and is cleared). An automatic write may never replace a non-empty `Zerodha_Basket_Buy.json` with an empty one: GO is transient (needs an open market and a <30 s tick), so mirroring it erased the owner's basket at 15:30:03 and again on a stale tick at 15:18, minutes before he had to buy. Only a real new order set or an explicit manual export may clear it.
9. **A no-signal day is correct behaviour, and hit rate is not the objective** (v1404). Never treat a day with nothing above the floor as a broken run, and never lower the floor to "get a trade in". Any floor/band change must be argued on account-level profit over the whole session set with the real capital (including idle days and the time cash is tied up), not on hit rate, mean per trade or a win streak. On the live Ensemble (v1497) lower floors earned as much per trade but tied cash up longer (5.3 h vs 2.1 h median hold), so 1.6 still won at account level; the 2.15+ maxed-out scores lost money and are skipped.

10. **Surveillance (REG1) does not cover upper-circuit locks, and never will** (v1407; the engine gained its own circuit filter in v1418, but REG1 still does not and will not cover this). 21 Sep FEDDERSHOL and PROTEAN were frozen at +20% with **zero shares on offer** and carried **no REG1 flag at all** - verified against the loaded file, not assumed. NSE publishes ASM/GSM/ESM for *sustained* abnormal behaviour; a band lock that happens today is in no surveillance column today. Never answer "why wasn't this filtered" by pointing at the surveillance table without checking the actual REG1 row first. A locked stock is **BLOCKED**, not score-penalised: it cannot be bought at any price, so it is not a weak buy.
11. **The backtest never modelled an empty offer side** (v1407). The +1.18%/trade replay filled every pick at the close, so it silently assumed fillability.
